    FAQ — Straight Answers, Before You Enquire

    SCOPE · TIMELINES · FILES · PRICING · PAN-INDIA EXECUTION
    What exactly does ArtViSiON deliver end-to-end?

    Everything from brand identity (logo, brand books, typography systems) to production-ready execution: flex/ACP/acrylic/LED signage, glow signs, exhibition stalls, packaging die-lines, vehicle wraps, VM kits and print artwork — one studio, one accountable point of contact.

    How fast can a project move from concept to site-ready files?

    Concept routes in 24–72 hours. Production artwork (die-lines, bleed, CMYK, material-specific) typically 3–5 working days per format family. Urgent retail rollouts are handled on a same-week sprint basis.

    Do you work with clients outside Indore?

    Yes — remote-first across pan-India and global: annotated PDFs, CMYK print files, DXF/CAD for fabrication, and vendor-coordination checklists so any local fabricator can execute without guesswork.

    How is pricing structured?

    Per-service bands published in the Pricing section (e.g. logo suites ₹4,999–₹14,999; 360° campaigns ₹14,999–₹49,999; flex ₹18–35/sq.ft, LED ₹900–2,200/sq.ft). Fixed quotes are issued against a scoped BOQ — no hidden line items.

    What are the payment terms?

    Milestone-based — 50 / 40 / 10 exactly as listed under Production Investment, always with a proper GST invoice.

    How many revisions are included, and do I get source files?

    Two design revision rounds are included. Source files (CDR/PDF) are handed over on final payment.

    Is the site visit really free?

    Within Indore city, yes. Outside Indore, nominal travel applies and is adjusted in your order.

    Is there a minimum order?

    Design starts at ₹499. Production follows material minimums — just ask on WhatsApp.

    Who installs the work?

    Supervised teams in the Indore region. Elsewhere: fabrication files, an installation guide and video-call supervision.

    Which material suits my board?

    We recommend by sunlight, viewing distance and budget during the recce — with physical samples.

    Can you redesign our existing brand?

    Yes — starting with a visual audit, keeping what works and fixing what leaks.

    Why one studio for both design and production?

    Because the file that designs the board is the file that builds it — nothing is lost in translation.
